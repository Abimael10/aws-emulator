The other open ecs__events__sqs issues (20261122T102325Z-ecs-events-sqs-stoptask-publishes-no-further-ecs.md, 20261120T181150Z-ecs-events-sqs-ecs-task-state-change-events.md, 20261122T004908Z-ecs-events-sqs-no-ecs-task-state-change.md, 20261110T163010Z-ecs-events-sqs-a-placed-fargate-task-publishes.md) all concern 'ECS Task State Change' only and do not cover the service-level detail-type below.

Cause 1 of 1: the 'ECS Service Action' detail-type is never observable, so neither a service's task-configuration failure nor a healthy service's steady state is delivered, and the per-service `resources` scoping that must withhold one service's failure from another service's rule cannot be exercised (failing check: ecs__events__sqs.xsvc-ecs-1-unpullable-image-task-configuration-failure-not-delivered-to-sibling).
AWS: a FARGATE service whose container image tag does not exist publishes to the default bus an envelope with source 'aws.ecs', detail-type 'ECS Service Action', detail.eventName 'SERVICE_TASK_CONFIGURATION_FAILURE', detail.eventType 'ERROR', detail.clusterArn equal to the cluster's ARN, resources exactly [the failing service's ARN], and detail.reason beginning with the fixed text 'CannotPullContainerError' and naming the image reference that could not be pulled (the variable part is the image/tag from the task definition in the request, here the non-existent tag); a healthy sibling service publishes the same detail-type with detail.eventName 'SERVICE_STEADY_STATE' and detail.eventType 'INFO', resources exactly [its own service ARN]. Because `resources` is scoped to one service, a rule scoped to the healthy service's ARN must receive its SERVICE_STEADY_STATE and must never receive the sibling's failure, even when that rule's detail.eventName filter lists SERVICE_TASK_CONFIGURATION_FAILURE. Smithy models CreateService (input serviceName @required, deploymentConfiguration, networkConfiguration; errors ClientException/ClusterNotFoundException/InvalidParameterException 400, ServerException 500) and the service's returned serviceArn, but says nothing at all about eventing: the envelope above is recorded/documented behavior only.
Emulator: the scenario is cut off by the harness — the replay reports 'Error: Check timed out after 60.0s' as a mismatch with no assertion reached. All three rules (bad-service-scoped, good-service-scoped, and the near-miss scoped to the good service but filtering eventName on the failure) stay empty: CreateService is accepted and returns the predicted service ARNs, but no 'ECS Service Action' envelope is ever delivered to any target queue, so the check never passes its delivery barrier and never evaluates either the positive or the negative leg.
Why: a client that alarms on SERVICE_TASK_CONFIGURATION_FAILURE gets no signal that a service cannot pull its image and no SERVICE_STEADY_STATE for one that is healthy, so a permanently failing deployment is indistinguishable from a healthy one; and because every rule is silent, an absence can never be read as correct scoping — the negative contract (one service's failure not reaching another service's rule) is unverifiable rather than satisfied.
Fix: the ECS service scheduler path that records a task-configuration failure (CannotPullContainerError with the offending image) and a service reaching steady state, plus the ECS → EventBridge emitter for detail-type 'ECS Service Action' (detail.eventName, detail.eventType, detail.clusterArn, detail.reason, and the per-service `resources` scoping) in the ecs service crate's service/eventing component.
