## Description
Two net-new root causes under the `### s3__sagemaker` suite heading. They are bundled because they share one service heading; each must be closed separately. Numbered below: Cause 1 of 2 .. Cause 2 of 2.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Cause 1 of 2: the documented deploy-and-infer chain (CreateTrainingJob → poll DescribeTrainingJob to Completed → CreateModel over ModelArtifacts.S3ModelArtifacts → CreateEndpointConfig → CreateEndpoint → poll DescribeEndpoint to InService → InvokeEndpoint) produces no observable outcome inside a client-scale budget, so the check ends on the harness's 'Check timed out after 60.0s' instead of on any wire answer.
AWS: DescribeTrainingJob reports TrainingJobStatus and SecondaryStatus (both required enums) walking InProgress → Completed and ModelArtifacts (required) naming the written archive as S3ModelArtifacts; DescribeEndpoint reports EndpointStatus (required enum: Creating → InService) together with EndpointConfigName and ProductionVariants, so a poller terminates. For the inference itself the model says nothing — recorded behavior only: an XGBoost model trained with objective=binary:logistic answers a text/csv row with a single score in the closed interval [0,1], and the score of a row drawn from the label=1 feature region exceeds the score of a row from the label=0 region (the suite requires a separation greater than 0.2), which is what proves the weights behind the variant were fetched from the Model's PrimaryContainer.ModelDataUrl. Smithy: DescribeTrainingJob output TrainingJobStatus/SecondaryStatus/ModelArtifacts required; DescribeEndpoint output EndpointStatus required, EndpointConfigName, ProductionVariants; ModelDataUrl @pattern ^(https|s3)://([^/]+)/?(.*)$, max 1024.
Emulator: the whole flow never reaches its first assertion. Replay detail: 'Error: Check timed out after 60.0s' (60 s is the harness budget, not a requirement). The check's only waits are two status polls, DescribeTrainingJob and DescribeEndpoint, each with its own 900 s budget and a 20 s interval; every other call is straight-line. The clock-owed transition spans in the emulator are compressed, not wall-clock (services/service-com-amazonaws-sagemaker-sagemaker/src/state.rs: ENDPOINT_IN_SERVICE_AFTER = 300 ms, TRANSIENT_STATE_SETTLES_AFTER = that same value), so those spans alone cannot consume the budget. Hypothesis: either a status never becomes terminal at all (the poll spins), or the work performed synchronously inside a single handler before it answers — the in-process fit of the training run (services/service-com-amazonaws-sagemaker-sagemaker/src/state/training_run.rs, module docs lines 16-42: channels read out of the emulator's own S3 and a least-squares model fitted by batch gradient descent, bounded by MAX_FIT_OPERATIONS) or the serving path behind InvokeEndpoint — does not return inside a client's budget. The replay output does not isolate which call blocks; no per-call status, error code or message diverges, the flow simply never arrives.
Why: a client that deploys a model the documented way cannot complete the sequence at all. Everything downstream of a settled training job and a live endpoint — the artifact at S3ModelArtifacts, the variant→model→S3 resolution DescribeEndpoint reports, and the served probability itself — is unreachable, so the hosting surface cannot be used or verified end to end, and a caller cannot tell a stuck resource from a slow one because no terminal status and no failure reason is ever reported.
Fix: the training-job and endpoint lifecycle in the SageMaker state together with the work they own — the clock-owed settle instants and status materialisation in services/service-com-amazonaws-sagemaker-sagemaker/src/state.rs, the in-process run in src/state/training_run.rs, and the serving path in src/state/model_serving.rs reached from the sagemaker-runtime InvokeEndpoint handler. The intermediate statuses must stay observable in order while the whole chain settles inside a window an API client's poll loop can traverse, and a run that cannot do its work must settle Failed with a FailureReason rather than never settling.
Traces: s3__sagemaker.sagemaker-endpoint-config-model-data-url-resolution

Cause 2 of 2: an endpoint whose EndpointConfig carries DataCaptureConfig (EnableCapture=true, InitialSamplingPercentage=100, CaptureOptions Input+Output, CaptureContentTypeHeader.CsvContentTypes=['text/csv']) publishes no capture object beneath DestinationS3Uri after it serves an invocation, so the request/response pairing can never be read back.
AWS: Smithy (com.amazonaws.sagemaker) DataCaptureConfig — 'Configuration to control how SageMaker AI captures inference data'; EnableCapture (boolean, 'Whether data capture should be enabled or disabled'), InitialSamplingPercentage -> SamplingPercentage required @range 0..100, DestinationS3Uri required @length 0..512 @pattern ^(https|s3)://([^/])/?(.*)$, CaptureOptions -> CaptureOptionList required @length 1..32 with CaptureOption.CaptureMode a required enum (Input | Output | InputAndOutput), optional KmsKeyId and CaptureContentTypeHeader. For the delivery itself the model states nothing — recorded behavior only: with capture enabled at 100% sampling, AWS writes newline-delimited JSON capture files beneath DestinationS3Uri on a key path that carries the endpoint name and the variant name, and a record pairs captureData.endpointInput with the matching captureData.endpointOutput for the configured CaptureOptions (for a binary:logistic model the captured output is the same score in [0,1] the invocation answered).
Emulator: the check never reaches its capture assertions. Replay detail: 'Error: Check timed out after 60.0s'. Its one unbounded wait past endpoint readiness is a poll of ListObjectsV2 under the configured capture prefix with a 300 s budget and a 15 s interval, which returns early only once at least one key exists — a poll that necessarily outruns the harness budget when no object ever lands. Hypothesis: no capture object appears beneath DestinationS3Uri for the served invocation. A capture writer is present in the crate (services/service-com-amazonaws-sagemaker-sagemaker/src/state/data_capture.rs, module docs lines 18-31 state the write is best effort and is made as the deployment's execution role), so the divergence is the missing side effect rather than any refusal: InvokeEndpoint itself is answered 200, as AWS answers it.
Why: a Model Monitor, audit or drift consumer configures DataCaptureConfig and then reads the capture files to join the request and the response leg of each invocation; against this emulator it reads an empty prefix forever and cannot distinguish 'capture disabled', 'capture configured but silently dropped' and 'capture blocked by a missing s3:PutObject grant'. Nothing in the response surface reveals the difference, so a real misconfiguration and a correct deployment look identical.
Fix: the endpoint data-plane capture path — DataCaptureConfig handling in the CreateEndpointConfig/CreateEndpoint handlers and the endpoint state that records it, plus the capture writer in src/state/data_capture.rs invoked from the sagemaker-runtime InvokeEndpoint handler. For a sampled invocation it must write a capture object beneath DestinationS3Uri under a key carrying the endpoint name, each record pairing endpointInput with endpointOutput per the configured CaptureOptions.
Traces: s3__sagemaker.sagemaker-endpoint-invocation-output-captured-to-s3

Prerequisite: git_clone(), then run_behavioral_suite(); both checks below are replayed against the booted emulator.

Cause 1 — the deploy-and-infer chain never arrives:
1. s3:CreateBucket (with CreateBucketConfiguration.LocationConstraint outside us-east-1) and iam:CreateRole a role trusting sagemaker.amazonaws.com with AmazonSageMakerFullAccess.
2. PutObject a small CSV (label in the first column; label=0 rows with features in [0,1], label=1 rows with features in [3,4]) at train/train.csv.
3. sagemaker:CreateTrainingJob with AlgorithmSpecification {TrainingImage: the region's built-in XGBoost image, TrainingInputMode: File}, that RoleArn, one 'train' channel over s3://<bucket>/train/, OutputDataConfig.S3OutputPath s3://<bucket>/output/, ResourceConfig ml.m5.large x1, HyperParameters {objective: binary:logistic, num_round: 10}.

5. CreateModel over that artifact, CreateEndpointConfig with one ProductionVariant (ml.m5.large, InitialInstanceCount 1), CreateEndpoint, then poll DescribeEndpoint every 20 s — record how long EndpointStatus stays Creating.
6. Observe that the chain does not settle inside a client-scale poll window (the replayed check is killed at its budget), so InvokeEndpoint is never reached and the high/low score separation is never observable.

Cause 2 — capture never lands:
1. Do steps 1-5 above, but give the EndpointConfig DataCaptureConfig {EnableCapture: true, InitialSamplingPercentage: 100, DestinationS3Uri: s3://<bucket>/capture, CaptureOptions: [{CaptureMode: Input}, {CaptureMode: Output}], CaptureContentTypeHeader: {CsvContentTypes: ['text/csv']}}.
2. Once DescribeEndpoint reports InService, sagemaker-runtime:InvokeEndpoint once with ContentType text/csv and a feature row; it answers 200.
3. ListObjectsV2 under s3://<bucket>/capture/ repeatedly — observe that no capture object ever appears, instead of at least one .jsonl object whose key carries the endpoint name and whose record pairs captureData.endpointInput with captureData.endpointOutput.
